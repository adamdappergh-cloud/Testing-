# Church Bookkeeping Resources

## Knowledge — Church Accounting Fundamentals

- [Bookkeeping for Churches: How to Balance Faith and Finances — Foundation Group](https://www.501c3.org/bookkeeping-for-churches/)
  Practitioner-written overview: what a church bookkeeper does, the key tasks, and why fund
  accounting matters. Use for: orientation lessons and explaining the role to others.

- [Church Accounting: Ultimate Guide + Best Practices — Jitasa Group](https://www.jitasagroup.com/jitasa_nonprofit_blog/church-accounting-guide/)
  Comprehensive guide covering fund accounting, chart of accounts, required financial statements,
  and internal controls. Use for: Modules 1–5; one of the strongest free references available.

- [The Essential Church Bookkeeping Guide — Jitasa Group](https://www.jitasagroup.com/jitasa_nonprofit_blog/church-bookkeeping/)
  Practical breakdown of day-to-day bookkeeping tasks: data entry, deposits, invoices, bank
  reconciliation. Use for: Module 3 (recording transactions) and Module 4 (internal controls).

- [Fund Accounting for Churches — ChurchTrac](https://www.churchtrac.com/blog/fund-accounting-for-churches)
  Clear beginner explanation of what a fund is, why churches use fund accounting instead of
  profit/loss, and how to track funds separately. Use for: Module 2 (fund accounting lessons).

- [Accounting for Churches — Free Church Accounting](https://www.freechurchaccounting.com/accounting-for-churches.html)
  Lean, jargon-light guide with a worked example. Strong on the difference between single-entry
  and double-entry. Use for: Module 1 (foundations) and Module 3 (journals).

- [The Beginner's Guide to Accounting for Churches — Donorbox](https://donorbox.org/nonprofit-blog/accounting-for-churches)
  Covers the four required statements (Financial Position, Activities, Cash Flows, Functional
  Expenses), contribution tracking, and software options. Use for: Module 5 (financial reports).

- [Accounting for Churches — BooksTime](https://www.bookstime.com/articles/accounting-for-churches)
  Good on the Statement of Financial Position and Statement of Activities with example figures.
  Use for: Module 5 reference and building the annual report.

- [Church Financial Guidebook — Mississippi Baptist Convention Board (PDF)](https://mbcb.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/church-finanical-guidebook-resource.pdf)
  Baptist-specific; covers treasurer duties, offering procedures, segregation of duties, bank
  reconciliation, and budgeting. Directly relevant to a reformed Baptist context.
  Use for: Modules 3, 4, 6, and the governance lessons.

- [Deacons: Financial Stewardship and Voluntary Activity — Shepherd Thoughts](https://www.shepherdthoughts.com/baptistchurchny/deacons-in-the-church-financial-stewardship-and-voluntary-activity/)
  On the biblical and ecclesiastical basis for deacons managing church funds. Use for: Mission
  grounding and Module 7 (deacon's fiduciary duty).

## Knowledge — Free Templates and Spreadsheets

- [Free Church Accounting Spreadsheets — Free Church Accounting](https://www.freechurchaccounting.com/freespreadsheets.html)
  Downloadable Excel/Google Sheets templates: general ledger, offering record, cash disbursements.
  Use for: Module 3 — give the user working templates to start with immediately.

- [Free Church Accounting Excel Templates — Aplos](https://www.aplos.com/academy/free-church-accounting-excel-templates-and-pdf-downloads)
  Balance sheet template, chart of accounts template, and contribution tracking sheet.
  Use for: Module 2 (chart of accounts setup) and Module 5 (financial position statement).

- [Free Church Chart of Accounts Template — Jitasa Group](https://www.jitasagroup.com/nonprofit-resources/nonprofit-free-downloads/church-chart-of-accounts-template/)
  A downloadable, pre-filled chart of accounts template for churches. Use for: Module 2 —
  adapt this as the starting point for the church's own chart of accounts.

## Knowledge — Ghana Context

- [GRA Practice Note on Charitable Organisations — Ghana Revenue Authority (PDF)](https://gra.gov.gh/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Practice-Note-on-Charitable-Organisation.pdf)
  The GRA's own document on tax exemptions and obligations for registered charitable
  organisations in Ghana. Use for: Module 7 (Ghana regulatory context).

- [How to Register an NGO or Non-Profit in Ghana — Firmus Advisory](https://firmusadvisory.com/register-ngo-in-ghana/)
  Explains Companies Limited by Guarantee registration and annual filing requirements.
  Use for: Module 7 (legal standing of the church and annual returns).

- [Accountability and Internal Control in Religious Organisations: Methodist Church Ghana
  — ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/305816575_Accountability_and_internal_control_in_religious_organisations_a_study_of_Methodist_church_Ghana)
  Academic study of financial controls in a Ghanaian church. Identifies common weaknesses
  (blind trust, limited financial literacy, weak receipts). Use for: Module 4 — context and
  motivation for why controls matter specifically in Ghana.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/churchfinances](https://reddit.com/r/churchfinances) — Small subreddit; practical questions
  from church treasurers and bookkeepers. Use for: troubleshooting specific situations.

- [ChurchTrac Blog](https://www.churchtrac.com/blog/) — Regularly updated practical guides from
  a church management software team. Grounded in real small-church experience.

- Local: The Ghana Baptist Convention likely has a finance secretary or denominational treasurer
  who can advise on local practice and relationships with GRA. Worth contacting.

## Gaps

- No strong free resource found specifically for **double-entry bookkeeping for churches in
  Africa or Ghana** — the Ghana/Africa-specific academic papers are behind paywalls.
  Bridging from US/UK church guides to the Ghana context is done via Module 7.
- A **worked example of a Ghanaian church's annual accounts** in GHS would be ideal;
  none found freely available. The capstone lesson will synthesize one from scratch.
