# Curriculum: Church Bookkeeping

**Status:** Draft — awaiting approval
**Target capability:** Keep accurate, transparent financial records for a small reformed Baptist
church in Accra, Ghana — weekly recording, monthly reconciliation, monthly elder reports, and an
annual congregational financial statement.
**Based on:** CONCEPT-MAP.md (as of 2026-06-26)
**Estimated effort:** 19 lessons across 7 modules, ~10 weeks at 2 lessons/week
**Priority note:** Modules 1–4 make you operational immediately; Modules 5–7 complete the picture.
The first four modules are the urgent path.

---

## Module 1 · Foundations  _(~3 lessons)_
*Outcome: You can explain why church bookkeeping exists, state the accounting equation, and
correctly identify whether a transaction is a debit or credit to the right account type.*
*Concepts: C1, C2, C3, C4, C5*

1. **Lesson 0001 — Why Church Bookkeeping Matters** → C1, C2
   _Win:_ You can explain to any elder or member why accurate records are an act of stewardship,
   and why church accounting is different from keeping a shop's cash book.

2. **Lesson 0002 — The Accounting Equation** → C3, C4
   _Win:_ You understand that every transaction has two sides and can keep the books balanced.

3. **Lesson 0003 — Debits and Credits** → C5
   _Win:_ Given any transaction, you can state which account is debited and which is credited.

**Mastery checkpoint M1:** Given a list of 10 transactions (tithes received, utility bill paid,
donation to building fund, etc.), correctly write the debit and credit entries for each — without
looking at notes.

---

## Module 2 · Chart of Accounts & Fund Accounting  _(~4 lessons, needs M1)_
*Outcome: You have a working chart of accounts for the church, with at least three funds
tracked separately.*
*Concepts: C6, C7, C8, C9, C10, C11, C12, C13, C14, C15*

4. **Lesson 0004 — The Chart of Accounts** → C6, C7, C8, C9
   _Win:_ You can read and navigate the church's chart of accounts and know which "bucket"
   every transaction belongs to.

5. **Lesson 0005 — Income and Expense Categories** → C10, C11
   _Win:_ You can name every income source and expense category the church has, and assign
   each to the right account.

6. **Lesson 0006 — What Is a Fund?** → C12, C13
   _Win:_ You can explain the General Fund in plain language to a new committee member.

7. **Lesson 0007 — Restricted Funds** → C14, C15
   _Win:_ You can set up separate columns (or sheet tabs) for the Building Fund, Missions
   Fund, and Benevolence Fund, and explain why they must never be mixed.

**Mastery checkpoint M2:** Set up a chart of accounts spreadsheet for the church with at least
3 funds, and categorize 15 sample transactions into the correct accounts and funds — all from
memory, then verified against the template.

---

## Module 3 · Recording Transactions  _(~4 lessons, needs M2)_
*Outcome: You can process a full week's transactions — offerings, expenses, deposits — without
error, using the correct journals.*
*Concepts: C16, C17, C18, C19, C20, C21, C22*

8. **Lesson 0008 — Counting and Recording the Offering** → C16, C17, C18
   _Win:_ You can run the Sunday offering count procedure start to finish: count sheet, two
   signatures, giving record entries, deposit slip.

9. **Lesson 0009 — Recording Expenses** → C19, C20
   _Win:_ You never pay a bill without a voucher, and you record every payment in the
   disbursements journal correctly.

10. **Lesson 0010 — The Cash Receipts Journal** → C21
    _Win:_ You can enter all income for a week into the receipts journal, allocated by fund.

11. **Lesson 0011 — Petty Cash** → C22
    _Win:_ You can set up and manage a petty cash float, reconcile it, and replenish it correctly.

**Mastery checkpoint M3:** Process a simulated month of transactions (4 Sundays of offerings,
6 expense payments, 1 petty cash replenishment) in the correct journals — balanced to zero errors.

---

## Module 4 · Internal Controls  _(~2 lessons, needs M3)_
*Outcome: The church has written procedures that ensure no single person can mishandle funds
undetected, and you can reconcile the bank account each month.*
*Concepts: C23, C24, C25, C26*

12. **Lesson 0012 — Segregation of Duties & Authorization** → C23, C24, C25
    _Win:_ You can draw the church's duty-separation chart and draft a one-page authorization
    policy for the elders to adopt.

13. **Lesson 0013 — Bank Reconciliation** → C26
    _Win:_ You can complete a bank reconciliation for any month: find every difference, list
    outstanding items, and arrive at a reconciled balance.

**Mastery checkpoint M4:** Complete a bank reconciliation from a simulated bank statement and
ledger that contains 3 deliberate discrepancies — find and explain all three.

---

## Module 5 · Financial Reports  _(~3 lessons, needs M3 & M4)_
*Outcome: You can produce and present a monthly income-and-expenditure report and an annual
Statement of Financial Position.*
*Concepts: C27, C28, C29, C30*

14. **Lesson 0014 — Statement of Activities** → C27
    _Win:_ You can produce a monthly income-and-expenditure report, broken down by fund,
    ready to hand to the elders.

15. **Lesson 0015 — Statement of Financial Position** → C28
    _Win:_ You can produce an annual balance sheet showing what the church owns, owes, and
    holds in each fund.

16. **Lesson 0016 — Budget vs. Actual & Contribution Statements** → C29, C30
    _Win:_ You can add a budget-vs-actual column to the monthly report, and produce a member
    contribution statement at year end.

**Mastery checkpoint M5:** From a provided trial balance, produce a complete Statement of
Activities and Statement of Financial Position for the month — formatted, labelled, and ready
to present to elders.

---

## Module 6 · Budgeting  _(~2 lessons, needs M5)_
*Outcome: You can lead the annual budget process and monitor it monthly.*
*Concepts: C31, C32*

17. **Lesson 0017 — Building the Annual Budget** → C31
    _Win:_ You can draft a 12-month income-and-expenditure budget from prior-year actuals,
    and present it to the elders for adoption.

18. **Lesson 0018 — Monitoring and Variance Analysis** → C32
    _Win:_ You can read the budget-vs-actual report each month, identify variances that need
    action, and write a one-paragraph commentary for the elders.

**Mastery checkpoint M6:** Draft a full annual budget for a simulated small church using
provided historical data, present a rationale for each line, and flag two areas of risk.

---

## Module 7 · Ghana Context & Governance  _(~3 lessons, needs M5)_
*Outcome: You understand the church's legal and regulatory obligations in Ghana and can present
annual accounts to the congregation.*
*Concepts: C33, C34, C35, C36*

19. **Lesson 0019 — GRA & Legal Requirements** → C33, C34
    _Win:_ You know what the GRA requires of a charitable organisation in Ghana, what is
    exempt, and what must be filed annually.

20. **Lesson 0020 — Reporting to Elders & Congregation** → C35
    _Win:_ You can prepare and deliver the annual financial report at the AGM in plain
    language that any member can follow.

21. **Lesson 0021 — The Deacon's Fiduciary Duty** → C36
    _Win:_ You can articulate the biblical and practical basis of your responsibility, and
    explain to a new deacon what it means in day-to-day terms.

**Mastery checkpoint M7:** Produce a complete set of annual accounts for a simulated church
year — Statement of Activities, Statement of Financial Position, and a one-page summary for
the congregation — and present it as if at an AGM.
