# Mission: Form-Making for the Pokuase Climate

## Why
Having learned to read and organise a plan (the **Space Planning** series), the next step is **form** — the three-dimensional shape of the building. This is a *hands-on, generative* series: the goal is to **explore building-form ideas that answer the climate of the Pokuase site** — sun, breeze, rain, heat — and to do it freely, **not constrained by the existing concept plan**. The aim is to arrive at the design conversation with form ideas of one's own, born of the site.

## Success looks like
- Turn a climate fact (the SW breeze, the hot west sun, the downpours) into a deliberate **form move**.
- Generate and compare several climate-responsive **massing and roof concepts** for the site.
- Judge a form by how well it handles sun, breeze and rain — not only how it looks.
- Sketch and present form options to an architect as a *starting point*, not a critique.

## Context (the governing lens)
- Site: **Pokuase, Greater Accra, Ghana** — hot, humid tropics, ~6°N. Prevailing **south-west monsoon** breeze; high overhead sun; hot low east/west sun (west worst); heavy seasonal rain; Harmattan (NE, Dec–Feb).
- **Climate is the generator of form**, not a constraint added later.
- Metric (SI) throughout.
- **Exploratory, not plan-bound** — we deliberately set the existing concept plan aside and design from the site's forces.

## How this series works (same spine as Space Planning)
- **Layered lessons** — Core → Deeper → Apply, each gated by a checkpoint quiz, with a "you can stop here" marker.
- **Grounded in masters** — climate-form thinkers and tropical architects (Victor Olgyay, Hassan Fathy, Geoffrey Bawa, Glenn Murcutt, Le Corbusier's brise-soleil; Francis Ching for the formal vocabulary).
- **Hands-on** — every lesson ends in a thing you *make*: a force-map, roof studies, massing options, a section, a parti. The series builds toward a small portfolio of explored form concepts for the site.

## Syllabus (working)
1. **Climate as the first move** — read the site's forces (sun, breeze, rain, heat) and turn each into a form move. *(Olgyay, Fathy, Murcutt)*
2. **The roof as primary form** — overhangs, pitch, double/ventilated and parasol roofs for shade, rain and stack ventilation. *(Bawa, Fathy, Murcutt)*
3. **Massing for breeze and shade** — thin bars, pavilions, stilts, courtyards, self-shading; additive & subtractive form. *(Ching, Fathy, vernacular)*
4. **Dissolving inside and out** — verandas, breezeways, courtyards, screens / brise-soleil; form as a gradient of enclosure. *(Bawa, Le Corbusier)*
5. **From forces to a parti** — synthesise the moves into one or two coherent whole-form concepts and test them against sun and breeze. *(Kahn)*
- ★ **Capstone** — a small portfolio of climate-driven form concepts for the Pokuase site.

## Out of scope
- Structural engineering / calculations, costing, construction detailing.
- The internal room-by-room plan (that is the Space Planning series and the existing concept).
