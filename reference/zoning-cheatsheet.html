Zoning Cheat-Sheet

The three zones

  • PUBLIC living, dining, kitchen, guest WC — gather & receive
  • PRIVATE bedrooms, en-suites, study — rest & retreat
  • SERVICE laundry, utility, pantry, garage, storage — the working spaces

Three relationships that matter

  1. Buffer the private zone — never open a bedroom onto a public room.
  2. Keep traffic out of zones it doesn't belong in — don't route through a room.
  3. Place service where it serves — pantry/garage by the kitchen, laundry by the bedrooms.

Two quick tests

  • Night-walk: bed → front door — what rooms do you cross?
  • Colour the zones: three pens on the plan; look for a private room touching a public one.
