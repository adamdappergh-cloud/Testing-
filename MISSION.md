# Mission: Residential Space Planning

## Why
Building a new home and already working with an architect. Want to move from passive observer to active participant — able to read what the architect is showing, spot problems before they're built, and advocate for layout decisions that will matter in daily life.

## Success looks like
- Read a floor plan and immediately understand what's good or bad about it
- Sketch or describe a layout that works — right rooms in the right places, good flow
- Ask smart, specific questions in architect meetings rather than nodding along
- Confidently push back on design directions with reasoning, not just instinct

## Constraints
- Beginner — no prior architectural training
- Already in the process (working with an architect), so learning needs to be immediately applicable
- Plain language teaching preferred

## Out of scope
- Structural engineering, building codes, or construction methods (for now)
- Commercial or multi-unit design
- Interior design and finishes
